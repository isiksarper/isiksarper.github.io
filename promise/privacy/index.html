  • No account, no sign-in.
  • We run no servers. Nothing you type or say is sent to us.
  • Promise itself has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • Your promises are stored on your phone and/or watch. They travel only between your own paired devices, through Google Play services.
  • Promise works with a yearly, auto-renewing subscription (new customers get a 7-day free trial). Payment and the subscription are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. Google Play's billing library, which the app includes to make purchases possible, sends Google technical diagnostics (library version, device model, Android version — never your promises). See §5.

If you add the Promise Tile or complication on your watch, your next promise and today's count are shown on the watch face — visible to anyone who looks at your watch.

2. Voice input

Promise does not record audio and does not request the microphone permission. When you tap the microphone, Promise asks Android to open the speech recognition service installed on your device (Android's standard speech-recognition request). That service — usually provided by Google or by your device maker — listens and returns the recognized text to Promise. Depending on your device, language and settings, recognition may happen on the device or on the provider's servers; that processing is covered by the provider's own privacy policy (for example, the Google Privacy Policy). Promise receives only the resulting text. You can always type instead of speaking.

3. Understanding your sentence

Working out who, what and when from your sentence happens entirely on your device, without an internet connection and without any AI service. This automatic understanding currently supports English and Turkish. In the other app languages, your sentence is saved as written and you fill in the details yourself — the app never invents a person, date or time.

4. Sync between your phone and watch

If you use Promise on both a phone and a Wear OS watch, your promises (the fields listed in §1), deletions and your subscription status are synchronized between the two devices using the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play services. According to Google, this data travels over an encrypted Bluetooth channel or, when Bluetooth is not available, through Google's cloud end-to-end encrypted. Only the same app, signed with the same key, on your own devices can read it. We have no access to it.

If you use only one device, nothing is synchronized.

5. Subscription and Google Play billing

  • Subscription: Promise works with a yearly, auto-renewing subscription. New customers get a 7-day free trial, started through Google Play at the end of the welcome screens. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, the paid subscription starts and renews every year until you cancel. The price is shown by Google Play in your local currency before you subscribe and may vary by country. Subscription terms: Terms of Use.
  • Without a subscription: adding new promises requires an active subscription (including the trial). If your subscription ends, you can still view and complete your existing promises and your reminders keep working; only adding new promises is locked. No data is deleted.
  • Purchases and subscription management are handled by Google Play. Your payment details, billing address, subscription and order history are processed by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see them. To cancel: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • The app learns from Google Play, on your device, whether your subscription is active (the subscription product, its state and a purchase token). It checks this on the device and stores only the subscription status and the time it was last verified (§1). The purchase token is not stored, and none of this is sent to us. The app asks Google Play for the status each time it comes to the foreground, so renewals, cancellations, expiry, refunds and restores are picked up automatically. Without an internet connection the last verified status is used. You can use your subscription on a new device with Restore purchase using the same Google account.
  • Your watch receives the subscription status from your paired phone over the encrypted connection described in §4.
  • Google Play billing diagnostics. To make purchases work, the app includes Google's Play Billing Library. Whenever the app connects to Google Play billing (on start-up and when it comes to the foreground — also for users without a subscription), this library sends Google technical diagnostic data such as the library version, the app's package name, the device manufacturer and model and the Android build version. It contains no promises, names or other content from the app, and it is not sent to us. Google uses it to keep the billing service working and to fix errors; Google's privacy policy applies. This is also why the app declares internet access (§8).

6. What we do not do

  • No developer servers, no accounts.
  • Promise's own code contains no analytics, telemetry or usage statistics and no crash-reporting service. (The only diagnostics are those Google Play's billing library sends to Google, §5.)
  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.

7. Cloud backup

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Promise, so your promises are not copied into your Google account backup, and they are not moved to a new phone by Android's device-to-device transfer either.

This means: if you reset or lose your device and Promise is not installed on another paired device, your promises cannot be recovered — use Export data (§9) if you want your own copy. Your subscription is not lost — it belongs to your Google account; restore it from Google Play with the same account.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): to show reminders. You can decline; the app still works, you just won't receive reminders. Phone and watch each show their own reminder; when the Promise watch app is installed, the phone's reminder is not copied to the watch a second time.
  • Alarms & reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): so reminders arrive exactly on time. You can turn it off in Android settings; reminders then may arrive a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to set your reminders again after the device restarts (alarms do not survive a restart).
  • Google Play billing, internet access and network state (added by Google's Play Billing Library): to talk to Google Play for purchases and purchase checks, and for the library's diagnostics (§5). Promise's own code makes no network requests.
  • Other technical permissions added by Android libraries (for example keeping the watch awake for a moment while background work runs) do not give access to any personal data.
  • Promise does not request access to the microphone, contacts, location, camera, photos or files.

9. Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your devices until you delete it.
  • Export (phone): Settings → Privacy → Export data saves a JSON file with your promises and people names to a location you choose in Android's file picker. Once saved, that file is outside Promise's protection; if you choose a cloud folder, that service's terms apply.
  • Deleting a promise hides it immediately (you can undo).
  • Delete all promises (phone): Settings → Privacy → Delete all promises deletes every promise on the phone (after a confirmation) and sends the deletion to your paired watch.
  • So that deletions also reach your other device, a deleted promise is kept in the local database as a hidden deletion record (including its text), and the list of people names is kept. These records are not cleaned up automatically.
  • To erase everything completely on a device: Android Settings → Apps → Promise → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Do this on each device you use (phone and watch).
  • Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us with any question.

10. Children

Promise is a general productivity app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not collect data from anyone.

11. Your rights

Data-protection laws (such as the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK) give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Since all of your Promise data is only on your own devices, you can exercise these rights directly in the app (edit, export, delete) and in Android settings. For speech recognition, Google Play purchases and the billing library's diagnostics, please contact Google.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and the version linked from Google Play. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes. This policy applies together with the Terms of Use.

8. İzinler

9. Verilerin saklanması, dışa aktarılması ve silinmesi

  • Verilerin, sen silene kadar cihazlarında kalır.
  • Dışa aktarma (telefon): Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Verileri dışa aktar, sözlerini ve kişi adlarını içeren bir JSON dosyasını Android dosya seçicisinde senin seçtiğin yere kaydeder. Kaydedilen dosya artık Promise'in korumasında değildir; bir bulut klasörü seçersen o hizmetin koşulları geçerlidir.
  • Bir sözü silmek onu hemen gizler (geri alabilirsin).
  • Tüm sözleri sil (telefon): Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Tüm sözleri sil, onayından sonra telefondaki bütün sözleri siler ve silme işlemini eşleşmiş saatine de gönderir.
  • Silme işleminin diğer cihazına da ulaşması için silinen söz yerel veritabanında gizli bir silme kaydı olarak (metniyle birlikte) tutulur; kişi adları listesi de kalır. Bu kayıtlar kendiliğinden temizlenmez.
  • Her şeyi tamamen silmek için: Android Ayarlar → Uygulamalar → Promise → Depolama → Depolamayı temizle ya da uygulamayı kaldır. Bunu kullandığın her cihazda (telefon ve saat) yap.
  • Verilerinden hiçbirini tutmadığımız için bizim tarafımızda silinecek bir şey yoktur. Her türlü soru için yine de bize yazabilirsin.
