Privacy Policy

Fluxbot, a game by isiksarper (app id com.isiksarper.fluxbot). Last updated: 20 October 2026. Türkçe özet aşağıda.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • The game has no server of its own and no analytics. Nothing about you is sent to the developer.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and offers optional in-app purchases (Google Play Billing). These Google services process some technical data, described below.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (best distance, Bolts and Chips, unlocked skins, daily chest timer), settings (music, sound, vibration, language) and the list of products you own are kept in the app's local storage (localStorage) on your device. They never leave your device through the game. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. Purchases stay in your Google Play account and come back with "Restore purchases".

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad, limit how often it appears, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as device model, OS version, app version, language) and your interactions with ads. This processing is governed by Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

  • Interstitial ads may appear on the death screen (not in the first runs, and with a minimum time between ads). Rewarded ads (for example "Continue", "x2 Bolts", the daily chest) are always optional and started only by a button you press.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it): on first launch Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF) is shown. Ads are requested only after the consent flow is completed or when consent is not required. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • US state privacy laws (for example California): where required, the same Google form and "Privacy options" button let you manage your choices, including opting out of the sale or sharing of personal data for personalised advertising.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Privacy → Ads) and opt out of ad personalisation there.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers four optional one-time purchases: Remove Ads, 100 Chips, 600 Chips and Starter Pack. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; your payment details (card, address) never reach the game. The game only learns from Google Play on your device which products you own and whether a payment completed, acknowledges the purchase to Google Play, and keeps that information on your device. Data processed by Google Play is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. "Remove Ads" turns off pop-up (interstitial) ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

Cookies and tracking

The game itself uses no cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or analytics. The ad network's use of identifiers is described above.

Children

Fluxbot is intended for ages 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights and deletion

Because the game keeps no data about you on its own servers, there is no account to delete: uninstall the app or clear its data to remove everything stored on your device. For data processed by Google (ads, Play Billing), use the privacy tools in your Google account and the in-game Privacy options. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing; for data held by Google, please contact Google, and for anything else contact us below.

Changes

If a new third-party service is added to the game, this page will be updated and the data involved will be listed here.
